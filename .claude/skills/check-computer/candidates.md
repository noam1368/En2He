## 1. Tiberias, 5,350 ILS: i5-13600KF + RTX 3080

- **CPU:** i5-13600KF, cooled by an Arctic Freezer 360mm RGB AIO.
- **Motherboard:** Gigabyte Z790 Gaming X AX (DDR5).
- **RAM:** Team Group Elite Plus 2x16 GB DDR5-5600.
- **GPU:** Zotac RTX 3080 AMP Holo (the model exists in 10 GB and 12 GB versions).
- **Storage:** SanDisk Optimus 5100 500 GB Gen4 SSD, plus a 2 TB WD Black HDD.
- **PSU:** Antec Atom 750W Gold, modular.
- **Case:** Antec DF700 Flux, white.
- **Notes:** strongest GPU and platform so far. 350 ILS over budget, no GPU purchase needed. 13th-gen CPU, so check the BIOS has Intel's stability microcode fix. The SSD is small. The AIO is used, so check its age.
- **Ask:** 10 or 12 GB? Age and warranty? Used for mining? BIOS updated? Stress test in person.

## 2. Ashkelon, 2,700 ILS: i5-12400F + RTX 3060 Ti

- **CPU:** i5-12400F, cooled by an Antec A400 RGB.
- **Motherboard:** Gigabyte B660M Gaming (DDR4).
- **RAM:** 16 GB DDR4-3200.
- **GPU:** RTX 3060 Ti 8 GB.
- **Storage:** 512 GB NVMe SSD, plus a 480 GB SSD.
- **PSU:** Thermaltake 650W.
- **Case:** Antec CX300 RGB.
- **OS:** Windows 11.
- **Notes:** a good base for the plan of selling the 3060 Ti and adding an RTX 5060 Ti 16GB (about 3,000 ILS). That comes to about 5,700 minus whatever the 3060 Ti sells for.
- **Ask:** How many RAM slots, and how many are free? Exact PSU model and age? Age and warranty? Used for mining?

## 3. Bat Yam, 4,300 ILS: i5-14400F + RTX 3070 Ti

- **CPU:** i5-14400F (3-year warranty), cooled by a Cooler Master Hyper 212 3DHP ARGB.
- **Motherboard:** Gigabyte B760M DS3H DDR4 (4 slots; 3-year warranty).
- **RAM:** Corsair Vengeance 2x8 GB DDR4-3000 (3-year warranty).
- **GPU:** Gigabyte RTX 3070 Ti Gaming OC 8 GB. Used for 1 year, trade-in; 1-year warranty.
- **Storage:** WD Blue SN5000 1 TB Gen4 NVMe (3-year warranty).
- **PSU:** Antec 750W Gold, semi-modular (5-year warranty).
- **Case:** Antec CX300 ARGB.
- **OS:** Windows 11 Pro.
- **Notes:** everything is new except the GPU, and it comes with warranties. Same 8 GB VRAM limit as #2, but 1,600 ILS more; the extra buys warranty, a newer CPU, a 1 TB SSD and a better PSU. Upgrading to a 16 GB card would cost about 7,300 minus the 3070 Ti's resale, well over budget. 14th-gen CPU, so check the BIOS fix.
- **Ask:** Is the seller a store or a private person (who honors the warranties)? Is the BIOS updated? Is the GPU warranty in writing?
